# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

First early-access source/developer release. Core acceptance: **READY WITH
NON-BLOCKING DEBT**. No production-signed installable binaries are included.

### Added

- Continue with ChatGPT and explicit API-key billing with protected credential handoff.
- Account-specific model discovery and watch-direct Responses streaming.
- Durable local chat history: reopen, continue, rename and delete.
- Real transport Stop and completed-only replay, including validated opaque continuation items.
- Phone-managed prompt presets and watch synchronization/insertion.
- Managed Photo Picker library, paired synchronization and multi-image prompts.
- Provider-native Thinking controls with exact supported strings and safe Default omission.
- API-key image generation, durable generated-image ownership and fullscreen zoom/pan viewer.
- Account/settings/sanitized diagnostics, developer guides and security/privacy documentation.
- GPL-3.0-only licensing with a narrow exact-version linking exception and third-party notices.

### Improved

- Round-screen, maximum-font, rotary, Markdown/code and explicit Latest reader behavior.
- Atomic persistence, recovery, cancellation, ownership and failure-path hardening.
- Integrated automated/emulator gates and owner physical core acceptance on Galaxy Watch 7.

### Known limitations

- #1: physical structured-table presentation remains absent; readable list fallback.
- #11: optional live API-key generation verification blocked by unavailable owner key;
  automated/emulator generation and viewer acceptance passed. SIWC generation unsupported.
- ChatGPT-plan Thinking intentionally exposes Default only; live API-key explicit effort
  was not physically tested (automated native contract coverage passed).
- Sent-image thumbnails are absent from completed transcripts.
- No cloud chat sync; unsent drafts/streaming state do not survive process death.
- TalkBack, funded API-key success and battery/runtime profiling are not physically verified.
